To manipulate novel objects from only a few examples, a robot needs representations that capture
what matters for the task: geometry, parts, keypoints, and semantics. We develop object-centric and
equivariant representations, such as neural descriptor fields and distilled feature fields, that
transfer skills across object instances and poses.

We also study 3D scene understanding from limited views, including completing cluttered scenes and
choosing which reconstruction model to use, so that downstream planners have the geometry they need.
